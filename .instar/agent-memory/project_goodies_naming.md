All component names in the-goodies ecosystem come from [The Goodies](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Goodies), a British comedy TV series (1970–1982):

- **FunkyGibbon** — from "Funky Gibbon", their 1975 comedy hit song (#4 UK charts)
- **KittenKong** — from the Goodies episode featuring a giant kitten rampaging through London
- **Inbetweenies**, **Blowing-Off**, **Oook** — all Goodies show references
- **The Goodies** — the repo name itself

**Why:** Don't puzzle over these names or try to find technical meanings. They're comedy references, not descriptive names. When explaining the project to someone new, mention the TV show origin.
